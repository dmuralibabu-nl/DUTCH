| Dutch | Pronunciation | English | When to use |
|-------|--------------|---------|-------------|
| Hallo | HAL-loh | Hello | Anytime (neutral) |
| Hoi | hoy | Hi | Informal |
| Hey | hey | Hey | Very informal |
| Goedemorgen | KHOO-duh-MOR-khun | Good morning | Before noon |
| Goedemiddag | KHOO-duh-MID-dakh | Good afternoon | Noon to 6pm |
| Goedenavond | KHOO-dun-AH-vont | Good evening | After 6pm |
| Goedenacht | KHOO-duh-NAKHT | Good night | Bedtime |

## 💬 Sample Conversations

### Conversation 1: Meeting Someone (Informal)
> **Anna:** Hoi! Ik ben Anna. Hoe heet je?
> **Mark:** Hallo! Ik heet Mark. Leuk je te ontmoeten!
> **Anna:** Insgelijks! Waar kom je vandaan?
> **Mark:** Ik kom uit Engeland. En jij?
> **Anna:** Ik kom uit Nederland, uit Amsterdam.

**Translation:**
> **Anna:** Hi! I'm Anna. What's your name?
> **Mark:** Hello! My name is Mark. Nice to meet you!
> **Anna:** Likewise! Where are you from?
> **Mark:** I'm from England. And you?
> **Anna:** I'm from the Netherlands, from Amsterdam.

### Conversation 2: At the Office (Formal)
> **Jan:** Goedemorgen. Mijn naam is Jan de Vries.
> **Lisa:** Goedemorgen, meneer De Vries. Aangenaam. Ik ben Lisa Bakker.
> **Jan:** Aangenaam, mevrouw Bakker. Hoe gaat het met u?
> **Lisa:** Goed, dank u wel. En met u?
> **Jan:** Heel goed, dank u.

**Translation:**
> **Jan:** Good morning. My name is Jan de Vries.
> **Lisa:** Good morning, Mr. De Vries. Pleased to meet you. I'm Lisa Bakker.
> **Jan:** Pleased to meet you, Mrs. Bakker. How are you?
> **Lisa:** Good, thank you. And you?
> **Jan:** Very good, thank you.

## 🔑 Cultural Notes

### Formal vs. Informal (je/jij vs. u)
- **je/jij** (you, informal) — use with friends, peers, young people
- **u** (you, formal) — use with strangers, elderly, in professional settings
- The Dutch are generally informal — when in doubt, many people say *"Zeg maar je"* (Just say "je")

### Three Kisses 💋
When greeting friends/family, the Dutch typically give **three kisses** on alternating cheeks (right-left-right). This is more common in social settings, not business.

### Common Dutch Names
**Men:** Jan, Pieter, Willem, Henk, Bas, Daan, Sem
**Women:** Anna, Femke, Sanne, Fleur, Emma, Lotte, Sophie
