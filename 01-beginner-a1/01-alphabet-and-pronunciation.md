# 🔤 Alphabet & Pronunciation

## The Dutch Alphabet

Dutch uses the same 26 letters as English, but the pronunciation is quite different!

| Letter | Dutch Name | Sound | Example Word | Meaning |
|--------|-----------|-------|-------------|---------|
| A | aa | "ah" (open) | **a**ppel | apple |
| B | bee | like English | **b**oek | book |
| C | see | "s" before e/i, "k" elsewhere | **c**entrum | center |
| D | dee | like English (soft "t" at end) | **d**ag | day |
| E | ee | "ay" or "uh" (unstressed) | **e**ten | to eat |
| F | ef | like English | **f**iets | bicycle |
| G | gee | guttural "kh" sound | **g**oed | good |
| H | ha | like English (softer) | **h**uis | house |
| I | ie | "ee" sound | **i**js | ice |
| J | jee | like English "y" | **j**a | yes |
| K | ka | like English (no aspiration) | **k**at | cat |
| L | el | like English | **l**and | land |
| M | em | like English | **m**an | man |
| N | en | like English | **n**ee | no |
| O | oo | "oh" sound | **o**ot | ear (of animal) |
| P | pee | like English | **p**ark | park |
| Q | kuu | always with "u" | **q**uiz | quiz |
| R | er | rolled or guttural | **r**ood | red |
| S | es | like English | **s**toel | chair |

## Special Dutch Sounds

### The G Sound 🗣️
The most famous Dutch sound! It's a guttural sound made in the back of the throat.

- **Hard G** (Northern Netherlands): like clearing your throat → *goed*, *groot*
- **Soft G** (Southern Netherlands/Belgium): softer, more like German "ch" → same words, softer

### Double Vowels
| Spelling | Sound | Example | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|---------|
| aa | long "ah" | m**aa**n | moon |
| ee | long "ay" | b**ee**n | leg |
| oo | long "oh" | b**oo**m | tree |
| uu | long "uu" | m**uu**r | wall |
| ie | "ee" | d**ie**r | animal |
| oe | "oo" | bl**oe**m | flower |
| eu | between "uh" and "oo" | d**eu**r | door |
| ui | "ow" (unique!) | h**ui**s | house |

### Diphthongs
| Spelling | Sound | Example | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|---------|
| ei / ij | "ay" (same sound!) | tr**ei**n / r**ij**den | train / to drive |
| ou / au | "ow" | h**ou**d / bl**au**w | hold / blue |
| aai | "ah-ee" | dr**aai**en | to turn |
| ooi | "oh-ee" | m**ooi** | beautiful |
| oei | "oo-ee" | **oei**! | oops! |

## Pronunciation Rules

### 1. Open vs. Closed Syllables
- **Open syllable** (ends in vowel): vowel is LONG → ma-**ken** (to make)
- **Closed syllable** (ends in consonant): vowel is SHORT → **man** (man)

### 2. Final Devoicing
Voiced consonants become voiceless at the end of a word:
- **d** → sounds like "t": hond → "hont" (dog)
- **b** → sounds like "p": heb → "hep" (have)
- **g** → sounds like "ch": dag → "dach" (day)

### 3. The Schwa
Unstressed "e" is pronounced as "uh" (schwa):
- *lopen* → "LOH-puh" (to walk)
- *de* → "duh" (the)

## 🎯 Practice

Try saying these words out loud:

1. **Goedemorgen** (good morning) → "KHOO-duh-MOR-khun"
2. **Alstublieft** (please/here you go) → "al-stoo-BLEEFT"
3. **Dankjewel** (thank you) → "DANK-yuh-wel"
4. **Scheveningen** (a beach town) → "SKHAY-vuh-ning-un"
5. **Gezellig** (cozy/pleasant) → "khuh-ZEL-likh"

> 💡 **Tip:** Listen to Dutch radio (NPO Radio 1) or watch Dutch YouTube channels to train your ear!
