# 📝 Daily Practice Plan

## Weekly Schedule

Spend **15–30 minutes per day** following this structure:

| Day | Focus | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| **Maandag** (Mon) | Vocabulary | Learn 10 new words + review old ones |
| **Dinsdag** (Tue) | Grammar | Study one grammar topic + exercises |
| **Woensdag** (Wed) | Listening | Dutch podcast or YouTube (15 min) |
| **Donderdag** (Thu) | Reading | Read a Dutch article or story |
| **Vrijdag** (Fri) | Writing | Write a short text (diary entry, email) |
| **Zaterdag** (Sat) | Speaking | Practice pronunciation or conversation |
| **Zondag** (Sun) | Review | Weekly review + catch up |

---

## Daily Warm-Up (5 Minutes)

Do these EVERY day before your main practice:

1. **Think in Dutch** — name 5 objects around you in Dutch
2. **Conjugate** one random verb in all persons
3. **Say the date and time** in Dutch
4. **Review** 10 flashcards from previous lessons

---

## Week-by-Week Plan (A1 Level)

### Week 1–2: Foundations
- [ ] Master the alphabet and pronunciation
- [ ] Learn greetings (hallo, dag, tot ziens)
- [ ] Practice self-introduction (Ik heet..., Ik kom uit...)
- [ ] Numbers 1–20
- [ ] Days of the week

### Week 3–4: Building Blocks
- [ ] Numbers 20–100
- [ ] Telling time (the "half" rule!)
- [ ] Months and dates
- [ ] Personal pronouns
- [ ] Verb "zijn" and "hebben"

### Week 5–6: First Conversations
- [ ] Present tense regular verbs
- [ ] Basic word order
- [ ] de/het articles (start memorizing!)
- [ ] Common everyday phrases
- [ ] Ordering food/drinks

### Week 7–8: Expanding
- [ ] Negation (niet/geen)
- [ ] Question words
- [ ] Adjectives (basic)
- [ ] Separable verbs intro
- [ ] Write a self-introduction paragraph

---

## Week-by-Week Plan (A2 Level)

### Week 9–12: Daily Life
- [ ] Daily routine vocabulary + separable verbs
- [ ] Shopping and food vocabulary
- [ ] Past tense (perfect) — regular verbs
- [ ] Past tense (perfect) — irregular top 20
- [ ] Hebben vs. zijn choice

### Week 13–16: Moving Around
- [ ] Directions vocabulary
- [ ] Public transport
- [ ] Describing people (adjectives + comparatives)
- [ ] Simple past tense
- [ ] Write a diary entry about your day

---

## Week-by-Week Plan (B1 Level)

### Week 17–22: Complex Communication
- [ ] Expressing opinions (Ik vind dat...)
- [ ] Subordinate clauses (verb to end)
- [ ] Future tense (zullen, gaan)
- [ ] Conditional (zou/zouden)
- [ ] Work and education vocabulary

### Week 23–28: Cultural Fluency
- [ ] Dutch culture and customs
- [ ] Complex sentence structures
- [ ] Relative clauses (die/dat)
- [ ] Linking words and connectors
- [ ] Write a short essay on a topic
