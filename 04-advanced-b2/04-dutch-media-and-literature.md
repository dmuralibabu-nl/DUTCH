## Understanding Flemish Dutch 🇧🇪

Belgian Dutch (Flemish) has some differences:

| Netherlands | Belgium | English |
|------------|---------|---------|
| pinnen | bancontact | pay by card |
| mobiel / telefoon | gsm | mobile phone |
| fiets | velo | bicycle |
| lekker | smakelijk | tasty |
| aardappel | patat | potato |
| alsjeblieft | a.u.b. | please |

**Pronunciation differences:**
- Softer G sound
- Different intonation (more musical)
- Different use of diminutives
