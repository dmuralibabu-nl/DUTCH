# 🌷 Dutch Culture & Customs

## Key Cultural Values

### 1. Directness (Directheid)
The Dutch are famously direct — they say what they mean without sugarcoating.
- This isn't rudeness — it's valued as **honesty**
- "Doe maar gewoon, dan doe je al gek genoeg" (Just act normal, that's crazy enough)

### 2. Gezelligheid 🕯️
The untranslatable Dutch concept of coziness, togetherness, and good vibes.
- A gezellig evening with friends
- A gezellig café with candles
- "Wat gezellig!" — the highest compliment

### 3. Tolerance (Tolerantie)
- Historical tradition of accepting different viewpoints
- "Leven en laten leven" (Live and let live)
- Progressive social policies

### 4. Equality (Gelijkheid)
- "Doe normaal" culture — don't show off
- Flat hierarchies at work
- Everyone cycles — even the prime minister!

## Social Customs

### Meeting People
- **Three kisses** on the cheek (right-left-right) for close friends/family
- **Handshake** for business and first meetings
- **Always on time!** Being late is considered rude
- **Agenda culture** — even friends schedule coffee weeks in advance

### Visiting Someone's Home
- **Always bring something** — flowers, wine, or chocolate
- **Take off your shoes** if the host does
- **Birthday circles** — sit in a circle, congratulate EVERYONE at a birthday party
- **Congratulate the family** — "Gefeliciteerd met je moeder!" (Congrats with your mother!)

### At Work
- **First names** are common, even with your boss
- **Meetings have agendas** and start on time
- **Borrel** — after-work drinks on Friday (very important for networking!)
- **Vergadercultuur** — the Dutch love meetings

## The Dutch & Their Bikes 🚲

- **23 million bikes** in a country of 17 million people
- Children learn to cycle before they can walk (almost!)
- Dedicated bike lanes (fietspaden) everywhere
- **Fietsenstalling** — bicycle parking garages at train stations
- It's normal to cycle in rain, wind, and even snow
- The **bakfiets** (cargo bike) is used to transport children, groceries, furniture...
