Words that look like English but mean something different — the most common traps!

## Tricky Pairs in Context

### 1. "Ik wil een brief schrijven"
❌ I want to write a brief → ✅ I want to write a **letter**

### 2. "De brand was verschrikkelijk"
❌ The brand was terrible → ✅ The **fire** was terrible

### 3. "Hij is heel slim"
❌ He is very slim → ✅ He is very **smart**

### 4. "Ik ga naar de winkel"
❌ I'm going to the wrinkle → ✅ I'm going to the **shop**

### 5. "Eventueel kunnen we morgen gaan"
❌ Eventually we can go tomorrow → ✅ **Possibly** we can go tomorrow

### 6. "Ik neem de trap"
❌ I'm taking the trap → ✅ I'm taking the **stairs**

### 7. "De weg is glad"
❌ The road is glad → ✅ The road is **slippery**

### 8. "Ze is een brave hond"
❌ She is a brave dog → ✅ She is a **well-behaved** dog

## Tips to Avoid False Friend Mistakes

1. **Never assume** a Dutch word means the same as an English lookalike
2. **Learn words in context** — see them in sentences, not in isolation
3. **Make a personal list** of false friends you encounter
4. **Practice with a partner** who can catch your mistakes
5. **When in doubt, look it up!** — Use Van Dale dictionary
